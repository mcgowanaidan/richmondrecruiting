Why families call us

Talent gets you in the conversation. Process keeps you in it.

The rules are not obvious

Eligibility, contact windows, divisions and scholarship types each have their own logic. Missing one costs options, and most families learn that after the fact.

Coaches get hundreds of emails

A clear subject line, a short film link and the right target list beat a long message sent to the wrong staff. Most players have never been shown how.

Offers need a second read

A scholarship, a preferred walk-on spot and a roster invitation are three different things. Families deserve a plain-English comparison before a decision.

The roadmap

Four stages. One direction.

Every player starts in a different place. The yard line is the grade you are in, and the work changes as you move toward the end zone.

  1. Own 20Grades 9–10

    Build the base

    The groundwork that keeps every door open.

    • Grades and core courses on track
    • Start a film library from game one
    • Learn how the levels differ
    • Attend camps to get an honest read
  2. 50Grade 11

    Get seen

    The year most outreach happens.

    • Register with the NCAA Eligibility Center
    • Build a target list by level and fit
    • Send coach emails with film attached
    • Camps and unofficial visits
  3. Red zoneGrade 12, fall

    Sort the offers

    Turn interest into clear choices.

    • Read every offer for what it really says
    • Plan official visits
    • Questions to ask staff and current players
    • Compare cost, role and academics
  4. End zoneSigning

    Commit with eyes open

    Finish the process the right way.

    • Understand signing paperwork
    • Close out recruiting courteously
    • Prepare for the transition to campus

About · Founder & CEO

Tyler Elrod

Founder & CEO, Fall Line Recruiting

Tyler spent more than three years with the University of Virginia football program as a Football Recruiting and Scouting Assistant, from December 2022 to February 2026. His responsibilities included overseeing weekly written reports, scholarship trackers, and score and statistic reports, along with game day setup and official visit assistance.

Before that, he was a two-year starting quarterback at Hanover High School, so he has seen recruiting from the player's side as well as the program's.

Tyler earned a bachelor's degree in Chemistry (2023) and a Master of Education in Intercollegiate Athletics Administration (2025), both from the University of Virginia, and studied at The Scouting Academy in 2024. He founded Fall Line Recruiting to give Richmond-area players and their families the guidance that is usually available only to those who already know the system. He teaches the process, reviews the work, and leaves the decisions to the player and his parents.
